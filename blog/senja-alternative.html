If you need a clean text testimonial wall on your site and don't want another monthly bill, this page is for you. If you need video testimonials, imports, or AI case studies, Senja is probably the better tool, and we explain why below.

What Senja costs in 2026

Senja has a generous free plan (15 video and text testimonials). The paid plans are Starter at $29/month (1 project, 3 forms), Pro at $59/month (5 projects, then $10/month for each extra), and Business at $179/month, a founding offer that's normally $249. Paying yearly gets you 2 months free.

Where Senja wins

  • Video testimonials, plus reels made from them. ProofWall v1 handles text and star ratings only.
  • Imports from many review sites and social platforms. ProofWall has no importer yet.
  • AI tools: testimonial analysis and generated case studies.
  • Verified testimonials (including Stripe-verified purchases), a Zapier integration, an API, and translations.
  • No server to run. You sign up and start.

Where ProofWall wins

  • Pay once: $59 for one site or $149 for unlimited client sites, with 1 year of updates. The app keeps working after that.
  • Your data, one file: testimonials live in a single JSON file on your server, so backups are just a copy.
  • No telemetry: the app doesn't phone home, and the widget loads from your own domain.
  • Approval first: nothing appears on your wall until you click Approve.

Moving from Senja

Senja lets you export your testimonials. ProofWall doesn't have a CSV import yet. It's on the roadmap, and we won't claim it until it ships. For now, add your approved testimonials by hand, or have customers resubmit through your new form. Only move over testimonials that real customers wrote and agreed to publish.
